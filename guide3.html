INTERACTING WITH PROTESTERS

ESTABLISHMENT'S EDITION

AVOIDANCE

The first control strategy you should take is avoidance. It's the cheapest way to deal with it: by not dealing with it at all. See no evil, hear no evil. Always look the other way, and see if the strategy works. If the problem disappears, perfect. No resources were used, no feelings were hurt, and our revenue would continue to flow smoothly.

Now when protesters take onto the streets, you still can use avoidance. Just look the other way. When they stand right by your office, just look the other way.

Now what if it's too hard to look the other way? What if their demands start to feel a bit reasonable, or that you'd like to bring back the peace and normalness before this whole problem even started?

SUPRESSION

You could use your iron fist. Your establishment was built upon one goal: to make the world a better place by creating the best version of your product.

So when people are protesting against technological advances that can simplify their lives and possibly even save it, sometimes you gotta do what you gotta do.

ADJUSTMENT

Sometimes, you give in. It's not terrible to give in, just a small scratch, that's all.

As long as you stay true to your core values, and you keep exercising control to create the best version of what you initially set out to create, you're golden.

Most established establishments refuse to partake in this route immediately, because if all demands were met, our resources and revenue would fall. Think about the families whose income relies on your product's success? That newspaper you wrote carries your daughter's smile at the dinner table. That financial aid system you spent hours developing developed a community of students who now have a common story to tell when they graduate.

Just, think about your reason for doing this.

CAPITULATION

People hate you. Like, HATE you. You tried everything. You tried to negotiate last minute. You tried to compromise and reduce their demands. You're running out of time. Now I am not a legal advisor, so I can't provide any legal advice, although I would recommend reaching out to purchase a Duck™ charm to sort out your finale regarding your establishment. Might as well, considering that it's game over.
