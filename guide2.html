FAME IS FORTUNE

You've heard of the buzzer words: the way a friend's eyes light up when they recognize the brand of a phone, or that famous restauraunt everyone tried to reserve a plate at.

With the first few years of starting your establishment, it'll be hard. Companies won't use your products: they'll test it, they'll scrutinize it, and many of your products and ideas would go unheard.

But with branding, this is a different story.
